Frequently asked questions.

Can I change my appointment?

Yes. Appointment changes can be made from your booking details before the scheduled visit.

What information do I need to book?

You only need your preferred care type, visit format, and date to start finding available appointments.

Is CarePath for emergencies?

No. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, contact your local emergency service immediately.
