Design question

How might booking care feel safe and understandable for more people?

01

Access barriers

Design for anxiety, jargon, cost uncertainty, low vision, keyboard use, and cognitive load.

02

One decision at a time

A three-step flow makes progress predictable and lowers memory demand.

03

Trust through content

Plain language and visible estimates reduce ambiguity before confirmation.
